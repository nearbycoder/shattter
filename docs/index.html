What's inside

20 crack patterns

Spider web, lightning, honeycomb, snowflake, spiral, tempered glass and more. Each tap leaves its own crack.

6 shatter animations

Explode, Crumble, Black Hole, Blast, Slow-Mo and Hang & Drop, with a Rewind repair that flies the pieces back.

Prank screens

Glass over a convincing fake lock screen, home screen, text thread, switched-off screen or photo. Then a dead-pixel reveal and “Gotcha!”.

10 sound packs

Phone screen, window pane, wine glass, car window, mirror, crystal, ice, cartoon, 8-bit and more, all synthesized on device.

Made for foldables

Bend the hinge and stress cracks spread along the fold. Crackgate mode recreates the famous crease crack the moment it opens.

Private by design

No accounts, ads, tracking or network access. Everything happens on your device.

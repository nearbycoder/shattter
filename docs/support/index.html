Frequently asked questions

How do I open the options?

Hold anywhere on the screen with two fingers for about a second. The options are hidden on purpose so pranks stay convincing.

How do I break the glass?

Tap it. Each tap adds a crack; after the number of taps set in the options (1 to 8), it shatters. Choose “Never” to keep a cracked screen. You can also shake the phone to smash it, or drag with two fingers to bend it.

How do I get the glass back?

Tap the broken screen, or turn on “Repair automatically” in the options. Pick a “Fade In” or “Rewind” repair animation.

How do the prank screens work?

In the options, choose Lock Screen, Home Screen, Messages, Screen Off or Photo. The glass sits over a convincing fake screen. When it breaks you'll see a dead-pixel effect, then an optional “Gotcha!” reveal. The fake screens never read any of your real data.

What is Crackgate mode?

A spoof of the October 2026 reports of foldable inner displays cracking along the crease. Turn it on, fold your phone and hand it to someone: when they open it there's a crunch, a crack down the crease and a band of stuck pixels. On other iPhones and iPads it triggers with a tap.

Why is there no sound?

Check that “Sound” is on in the options and the volume is up. Shattter plays even when the ringer switch is on silent. Try a different sound pack. Tapping a pack plays a preview.

Does it work on iPad and non-foldable iPhones?

Yes. Hinge effects need a foldable iPhone; everything else works on any iPhone or iPad running iOS 26 or later.

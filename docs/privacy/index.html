Privacy Policy

Effective October 27, 2026

The short version: Shattter does not collect, store on servers, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no tracking and no network connections. Everything happens on your own iPhone or iPad.

Information kept on your device

Shattter saves only your preferences (chosen screen, crack pattern, sound pack, shatter style and similar options) and a count of panes you have shattered. These stay in the app's private storage on your device and are deleted when you delete the app. We, the developer, never receive them.

Device sensors

Shattter reads the device's motion sensors to slide reflections across the glass, and, on foldable iPhones, the hinge angle to crack the glass when you bend it. These readings are used instantly to animate the screen and are never stored or sent anywhere.

Fake screens

The prank screens (lock screen, home screen, messages, photo) are drawings made by the app. They do not read your real notifications, messages, photos, contacts or any other data.

Children's privacy

Because Shattter collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13, there is nothing for us to access, share or delete.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics, social or tracking software, and it does not connect to the internet.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
